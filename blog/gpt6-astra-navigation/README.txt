Differential Robotics Team — bilingual technical blog

Open index.html in a browser, or serve the folder with a static web server.
English is the default. The EN / 中文 buttons switch the full article and
remember the selection. Direct links support ?lang=en or ?lang=zh.

The page contains three research sections, the introduction, closing thoughts,
eight figures, fifteen recordings and ten references. Figures and videos
are numbered separately in appearance order; references follow first citation.
The English article is pre-rendered and readable without JavaScript.

Content is stored in the ARTICLES object in index.html. Keep both language
versions and the initial English HTML synchronized when editing.
