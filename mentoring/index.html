mentoring & collaboration

Connecting research ideas with working robotic systems.

research directions

I mentor students working on embodied intelligence and robotics, with a focus on:

  • Robot foundation models: vision-language-action models, language-conditioned navigation, and generalization across environments.
  • World models: visual prediction, generative data, and learning representations that support embodied decisions.
  • Embodied robot swarms: coordination, collaborative perception, and decision-making across robot teams.
  • Robot learning and control: reinforcement learning, planning, adaptive control, and real-world aerial manipulation.

working together

I am interested in research that brings learning and physical interaction together—from model design and simulation to experiments on real robots.

If you would like to discuss a research idea or potential collaboration, email me with a brief introduction, your research interests, and links to relevant projects or publications.
